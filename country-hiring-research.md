# Remote Engineering Hiring: Country Research

> [!CAUTION]
> **⚖️ This document is not legal advice. Legal counsel must review any hiring decision before we act on it.**
>
> The engineering team compiled this from public sources as a first-pass country shortlist. Lawyers and HR **must confirm** the following before we act:
> - The employment or contractor structure in each country, and the risk that a contractor is reclassified as an employee
> - Tax, social contributions, and permanent establishment (PE) risk
> - IP assignment terms
> - Cross-border transfer of patient data and personal data
> - The US sanctions (OFAC) screening process
> - How our current Philippines staff are engaged
>
> Regulations are changing fast (for example Poland's July 2026 reform, a pending Brazilian Supreme Court case, and martial law in Ukraine). The content reflects the research date.

> Research date: 2026-10-15
> Scope: a US company hiring remote backend/data engineers, either full-time through an Employer of Record (EOR) or as contractors. Time zones are not a factor.
> Ranking principle: **hiring policy first** (legality, contractor risk, employer cost, termination flexibility, regulatory stability, sanctions and payments). Talent, English, and cost come second.
> Salary figures come mostly from EOR and recruiting vendors. Read them as orders of magnitude, not exact numbers.

## Executive summary

- **Top picks:** 🇷🇴 **Romania** and 🇧🇬 **Bulgaria**.
  - Both are EU members with stable rules and low employer costs.
  - Both have world-class algorithmic talent: Romania won 4 golds at IOI 2025, and Bulgaria won 2 golds and 2 silvers.
- **Next:** 🇵🇱 **Poland**, 🇮🇳 **India**, and 🇵🇭 **Philippines** (where we already have staff).
- **Conditional:** 🇺🇦 **Ukraine** and 🇬🇪 **Georgia**.
  - Contractor regimes are very simple in both.
  - Both carry geopolitical and sanctions-related risk.
- **Dropped:** 🇲🇽 **Mexico** and 🇧🇷 **Brazil**, because they are the most restrictive on policy. The supporting notes are in the appendix.
- **Full-time hires should go through an EOR** in every country. Using contractors for full-time, managed roles carries reclassification risk everywhere.
- **Sourcing:** our process already works like a contest, so contests are the best channel for reaching strong engineers. Details are under *Recommended next steps*.

### Points that apply to every country
- **IP must be assigned in writing.** In most countries, work a contractor creates does not belong to the client unless a contract says so.
- **Patient data:** every country has its own cross-border rules (GDPR and others). The simplest approach is to give engineers only de-identified or synthetic data.
- **Permanent establishment risk:** overseas engineers should never negotiate or sign contracts on the company's behalf.
- **Sanctions screening:** screen every hire against the OFAC SDN list and verify where they actually live. This matters most for Ukraine and Georgia.
- **Competitive pay:** to win top talent, expect to pay around the 75th–90th percentile of the local market.

## 1. 🇷🇴 Romania

**Why**
- 4 golds at IOI 2025, Romania's best result ever.
- English is #11 worldwide on EF EPI.
- About 205k IT employees in 2025. A wave of layoffs in 2026 has increased the number of available candidates.
- **Risk:** taxes change often. They rose through 2025–26, and contractor and micro-company rules are tightening.

**Policy**
- **EOR:**
  - The employer adds only **~2.25%** (work insurance). Employees pay most contributions: pension 25%, health 10%, and income tax 10%.
  - The IT income-tax exemption **ended in 2025**. Engineers' take-home pay fell, so expect pressure on gross salaries.
  - At least 20 working days of leave.
  - Probation up to 90 days (120 for managers). Notice is 20 working days (45 for managers).
  - Dismissal requires statutory grounds. There is **no universal statutory severance**.
- **Contractors (PFA sole practitioner or micro-SRL company):**
  - Very common in IT.
  - The tax code requires at least 4 of 7 independence criteria. **Working full-time and exclusively for one client is high risk.**
  - From 2026 micro-companies have a €100k revenue cap, a 1% rate, and must have at least one employee. The regime may be abolished in 2027 (unverified).
- **IP:** economic rights to software written by employees belong to the employer by default. Contractors need a written assignment.
- **Data:** GDPR applies. Transfers to the US need the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses, plus a data processing agreement (DPA).

**Pay (gross annual, employment):**

| Level | USD per year |
|---|---|
| Junior | $26–35k |
| Mid | $42–61k |
| Senior | $65–104k |

Levels.fyi gives a median of about $44k, a 75th percentile of about $63k, and a 90th percentile of about $86k.

**Platforms:** LinkedIn (main channel), eJobs, BestJobs, and devjob.ro. For algorithmic talent: olympiad alumni, infoarena, Kilonova, and pbinfo.

## 2. 🇧🇬 Bulgaria

**Why**
- 2 golds and 2 silvers at IOI 2025, unofficially 4th as a team (ahead of the USA) and Bulgaria's best result ever.
- English is #18 worldwide on EF EPI.
- **Joined the eurozone on 1 Jan 2026.** Payroll runs in EUR over SEPA, with no currency controls.
- **Risks:** a smaller pool (about 60k software professionals) and political volatility. The 2026 budget did not pass, and early elections were held in April 2026.

**Policy**
- **EOR:**
  - Employer contributions total about **19%**, but they apply only up to an insurable-income cap of €2,300 a month (from Aug 2026). For mid and senior engineers that brings the effective on-cost to about 5–10%.
  - Income tax is a **10% flat rate**.
  - At least 20 working days of leave, and no mandatory 13th-month pay.
  - Probation up to 6 months. Notice is 30 days by default and can be extended to 3 months by contract.
  - Dismissal requires statutory grounds or mutual agreement. Severance for redundancy is about 1 month.
- **Contractors:** all of these are common in IT.
  - A freelancer on a civil contract pays about 7.5% effective tax.
  - A sole trader is also an option.
  - A one-person company (EOOD) pays 10% corporate tax plus 5% on dividends.
  - The Labour Inspectorate can reclassify contractors under Labour Code art. 405a.
  - **Contracting through the engineer's own EOOD lowers the risk.**
- **IP:** software written by employees belongs to the employer by statute. Contractors need a written assignment that covers territory, term, and all uses.
- **Data:** GDPR applies.

**Pay:**

| Level | Local market (USD per year) | Remote for a foreign company / contractor |
|---|---|---|
| Junior | $18–32k | — |
| Mid | $29–57k | ~$48–66k |
| Senior | $43–99k | ~$62–96k |

**Platforms:** **DEV.BG** (the leading IT board, with an international employer portal), jobs.bg, and LinkedIn. For algorithmic talent: national olympiad and IOI alumni, and the Sofia and Varna mathematics high schools.

## 3. 🇵🇱 Poland

**Why**
- A strong competitive-programming tradition. The University of Warsaw won gold at the 2025 ICPC Central Europe regional.
- English is #15 worldwide on EF EPI, and the market is mature.
- **Risk:** the tightening of the B2B contractor route, described below.

**Policy**
- **EOR:**
  - Employer contributions are **~20.5%**, plus about 1.5% for the PPK pension plan.
  - 20 days of leave, rising to 26 after 10 years. There is no 13th-month pay.
  - Probation is at most 3 months. Notice is 2 weeks to 3 months depending on tenure, and dismissal requires a stated reason.
- **Contractors (B2B):**
  - Mainstream in IT.
  - **Since 8 July 2026, the Labour Inspectorate (PIP) can reclassify B2B contracts as employment by administrative decision**, without going to court. Fines reach PLN 60–90k, plus back-payments of social contributions and tax.
  - **Bottom line: hire full-time staff through an EOR, not B2B.**
- **IP:** employee software belongs to the employer automatically. A contractor assignment **must be in writing or it is void**, and it must list each permitted use ("field of exploitation").
- **Data:** GDPR applies.

**Pay (USD per year):**

| Level | Employment (gross) | B2B (invoice) |
|---|---|---|
| Junior | $26k | $29–39k |
| Mid | $45–49k | $55–61k |
| Senior | $70–77k | $80–81k |

**Platforms:** **Just Join IT** (largest IT board), No Fluff Jobs (salary range required), theprotocol.it, Bulldogjob, and LinkedIn. For algorithmic talent: alumni of the Polish Olympiad in Informatics, the Potyczki Algorytmiczne contest, and ICPC teams from Warsaw, Jagiellonian, and Wrocław universities.

## 4. 🇮🇳 India

**Why**
- About 5.8M tech workers, with English as the working language.
- The largest Codeforces and CodeChef population. Our rigorous testing should make screening high-volume applicants efficient.
- **Risks:** quality varies a lot (IT services versus product-company backgrounds), notice periods are 60–90 days, counter-offers are common, and attrition is high.

**Policy**
- **EOR:**
  - The four new **Labour Codes took effect on 21 Nov 2025**. Central rules followed in May 2026, and state rules are still rolling out.
  - Allowances above 50% of pay now count as "wages", which raises the base for provident fund (PF) and gratuity.
  - Employer cost is about **15–22%** (PF 12% plus gratuity about 4.81%).
  - Probation is usually 3–6 months.
- **Contractors:**
  - Common and well understood.
  - Exported services carry 0% GST under a Letter of Undertaking (LUT).
  - A US payer with no Indian presence has no withholding (TDS) obligation.
- **PE risk:** India rejects the OECD's 2025 home-office safe harbour.
- **IP:** contractor assignments must say **"worldwide, perpetual"**. Otherwise they default to 5 years and India only.
- **Data:** the DPDP Rules phase in fully by May 2027.

**Pay (product companies):**

| Level | USD per year |
|---|---|
| Junior | $9–23k |
| Mid | $14–40k |
| Senior | $40–70k+ |

**Platforms:** **Naukri** (largest, but noisy), LinkedIn, Instahyre, Cutshort, Wellfound, and AmbitionBox (company reviews). For algorithmic talent: CodeChef, Codeforces, ICPC regionalists, and IIT/NIT/BITS alumni.

## 5. 🇵🇭 Philippines (existing presence)

**Why**
- **We already have staff here**, so we can use referrals and existing admin and payment setup.
- English is #28 worldwide and #2 in Asia on EF EPI.
- **Risks:**
  - The talent pool skews toward outsourcing (BPO) and support roles. **Senior backend and data engineers are scarcer**, and US remote employers compete hard for them.
  - Contractors can be reclassified as employees.

**Policy**
- **EOR:**
  - Employer contributions: SSS 10% (capped), PhilHealth 2.5%, and Pag-IBIG 2% (capped).
  - **13th-month pay** is mandatory.
  - Probation is at most 6 months, **after which employees become regular automatically**.
  - Termination requires just or authorized cause and two written notices. Redundancy pay is 1 month per year of service.
  - Under the Telecommuting Act, remote staff must be treated the same as on-site staff.
- **Contractors:**
  - Common with foreign clients.
  - The **four-fold test** decides employment status. The most important factor is **control over the means and methods of the work**, and having the *right* to control is enough.
- **IP:** **a contractor owns the copyright unless a written agreement says otherwise.**
- **Data:** the Data Privacy Act treats health data as sensitive and requires breach notification within 72 hours.

**Pay:**

| Level | USD per month |
|---|---|
| Junior | $670–1,000 |
| Mid | $1,000–1,670 |
| Senior | $1,670–2,500 (foreign remote firms pay up to $4,500) |

To compete for strong seniors, budget **$2.5–4.5k a month**.

**Platforms:** referrals from current staff (highest-signal channel), JobStreet, LinkedIn, Kalibrr, and Indeed PH. For algorithmic talent: NOI.PH and ICPC Manila.

## 6. 🇺🇦 Ukraine (conditional)

**Why**
- About 303k IT specialists, a pool that stabilized in 2025 for the first time since 2022, with strong algorithmic depth.
- English is #45 worldwide on EF EPI.
- **Risks:**
  - Martial law and mobilization have been extended to 31 Oct 2026, and another renewal is likely. Most men aged 23–60 cannot leave the country and may be drafted.
  - Attacks on the power grid continue, so engineers need backup power and a second internet connection.

**Policy**
- **US sanctions (mandatory):**
  - Crimea and the so-called DNR and LNR are sanctioned regions. Occupied Zaporizhzhia and Kherson should also be treated as high risk.
  - Screen against the SDN list, verify residence, bank address, and IP location, and prohibit work from occupied areas by contract.
- **Contractors (FOP Group 3):**
  - The standard way for Ukrainian IT engineers to bill foreign clients.
  - Tax is 5% plus a 1% military levy, plus a minimum social contribution.
  - Under the IMF program, VAT for FOPs is expected from 2028. It has not been enacted yet.
- **Diia.City** (a special tax regime for IT) is open only to Ukrainian-registered companies. We could use it only through our own subsidiary or an EOR that is a Diia.City resident.
- **EOR:**
  - The employer pays 22% social contribution.
  - Employees pay 18% income tax plus a 5% military levy.
  - 24 days of leave.
- **IP:** a written assignment that lists the rights, scope, and payment, with a signed acceptance for each deliverable.
- **Data:** a GDPR-aligned bill is still pending. **Do not give engineers access to raw patient data.**

**Pay:**

| Level | USD per year |
|---|---|
| Junior | ~$11k |
| Middle | ~$30k |
| Senior | ~$54k |
| Tech lead | ~$66k |

These are DOU survey medians. A realistic US-client budget for a senior contractor is **$50–75k a year**.

**Platforms:** **Djinni** (free to post, pay only on hire), **DOU**, and LinkedIn.

**Policy score:** moderate, about 6/10.

## 7. 🇬🇪 Georgia (conditional)

**Why**
- About 25k people work in IT services. The sector grew fast in 2022–23 as Russian and Belarusian engineers relocated there.
- English is #35 worldwide on EF EPI.
- **Risks:**
  - The relocated engineers are leaving, and IT export revenue fell 12%.
  - Algorithmic depth is modest.
  - Politics: the foreign-influence law, a frozen EU accession process, mass protests, **US sanctions on the ruling party's founder**, and the EU suspending visa-free travel for Georgian officials.
  - Private IT firms and ordinary banks are not targeted today.

**Policy**
- **Contractors (Individual Entrepreneur with Small Business status):**
  - **1% tax on turnover** up to GEL 500k.
  - Very common for remote work with foreign clients.
  - Reclassification risk is low to moderate.
- **EOR:**
  - 20% flat income tax and **no social security**. Pension contributions are 2% from the employer and 2% from the employee.
  - 24 working days of leave.
  - Probation up to 6 months.
- **Russian or Belarusian nationals:**
  - They are not sanctioned by nationality, but we must screen them against the SDN list.
  - OFAC prohibits providing IT services to people *located in Russia*, so block access from Russia by contract.
- **IP:** contractors need a written assignment. **Data:** a new data protection law has applied since 2024.

**Pay:**

| Level | USD per year (remote) |
|---|---|
| Junior | ~$15–25k |
| Mid | ~$30–50k |
| Senior | ~$50–80k |

**Platforms:** jobs.ge, hr.ge, LinkedIn, and relocation-community Telegram channels.

**Policy score:** moderate. It is the easiest country on paper, but riskier than the EU options.

## Recommended next steps

1. **First cohort:** open roles in **Romania and Bulgaria** (EU, most stable policy, strong algorithmic talent), **India** (volume), and the **Philippines** (existing presence). Then compare talent quality using real R0–R2 pass rates and scores.
2. **Source through contests.**
   - Our process already works like a contest, and contest channels attract the strongest algorithmic engineers.
   - One option is to sponsor a Codeforces round. Each round draws about 15–25k registrants and reaches India and Eastern Europe at once.
   - Another is to follow Telegram's model and run our own prize contest that feeds directly into R0.
   - Details are in [sourcing-platforms.md](sourcing-platforms.md).
3. **If we need contractors,** Ukraine has the best talent. We should set up sanctions screening and operational contingency plans first.
4. **HR:** request quotes for these countries from 2–3 EOR providers (for example Deel, Remote, Rippling, Oyster).
5. **Legal:** confirm the contractor agreement template (including IP assignment), the cross-border rules for patient data, and whether our current Philippines engagement model needs adjusting.

## Appendix: countries removed from the ranking

### A. 🇲🇽 Mexico
- **Talent:** a 390k–800k pool and a solid competitive-programming pipeline (the OMI olympiad and ICPC Mexico). However, **English is very weak** (EF EPI #103).
- **Policy:**
  - Employment is presumed whenever work is personal and directed.
  - There is no at-will termination. Unjustified dismissal costs 3 months' pay plus 20 days per year of service.
  - Employers must share 10% of profits with employees (PTU).
  - Remote-work rules (NOM-037) require the employer to provide equipment and share utility costs.
  - The work week is falling from 48 to 40 hours between 2027 and 2030.
  - EOR on-cost is about 25–40%.
- **Pay:** seniors earn about $66–90k (vendor figures).

### B. 🇧🇷 Brazil
- **Talent:** about 6.9M GitHub accounts (#4 worldwide) and a strong ICPC tradition. English is weak (EF EPI #75).
- **Policy:**
  - Total employment cost is about **1.5× base salary**, including the 13th salary and the one-third vacation bonus.
  - Dismissal triggers a 40% penalty on the employee's FGTS severance fund.
  - **The legality of contractor (PJ) arrangements is pending at the Supreme Court (Tema 1389).**
  - Cross-border data transfers must use the regulator's (ANPD) standard clauses without modification.
- **Pay:** seniors earn about $54–110k (sources vary widely).
